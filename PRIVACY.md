## The short version

Focus Agent runs on your device. Your assignments, focus sessions, streaks,
notes, and chat history stay in your browser's local storage. We don't have
a server that collects your data. We can't see your assignments, your
grades, or anything else — and we can't sell what we never see.

## What Focus Agent reads

- **Screenshots you take on purpose.** The 📸 button (and 🖍 on pages that
  can't be highlighted, like Google Docs) takes a picture of the tab you're
  looking at, only when you tap it, and only the region you drag. That image
  goes to the coach's brain (your local bridge, or the hosted bridge and its
  AI provider) once, so it can explain the page. It is not kept: only a small
  thumbnail stays in that assignment's chat on your device. Nothing is ever
  captured in the background.

- **Your school portal** (Blackbaud, Canvas, or Google Classroom), using the
  login already in your browser. This happens on your device. Focus Agent
  never sees or asks for your portal password, and your login cookies never
  leave your browser. Starting with v0.6 it reads more than the assignment
  list so the coach can actually help: your **class list and current
  grades**, **graded assignment scores**, **class schedule**, teacher-posted
  **topics/units**, and the **instructions text** of each assignment. All of
  it is stored only on your device (see below).
- **Tab addresses during a focus session you started**, checked on your
  device against a built-in list of distracting sites, so your coach can
  nudge you. This checking stops when your session ends. Sites you visit
  are not logged by name anywhere except your own device's session stats.

## What Focus Agent stores (on your device only)

Focus sessions and their timing, streaks and XP, per-assignment notes and
time estimates, page annotations you draw, your chat messages with the
coach, and a cached copy of your assignment list. All of it lives in
Chrome's local extension storage. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

## What Focus Agent sends off your device

**Nothing.** The current version has no server, no analytics, no telemetry,
no crash reporting, and no accounts.

[FUTURE — AI backend] When AI coaching features are enabled, only what a
tutor would need to answer your request is sent: assignment titles,
instructions, and due dates; your course names and current grades as
numbers; your class schedule times; session statistics; and — **only when
you use Pre-check and paste it yourself** — the draft you want feedback on.
Never your name, student id, email, teachers' contact details, calendar
feed link, or login. These are
sent to our server, which forwards them to an AI provider (Anthropic) to
generate the coaching response, and returns it to you. Our server does not
store your data after answering; it is processing, not collection. This
section will be finalized before any such version ships.

## Students and parents

Focus Agent is built for students, including students under 13. Because the
current version collects nothing and has no accounts, there is no personal
information for us to hold. If a future version adds accounts or any data
collection, we will require verifiable parental consent for users under 13
(COPPA) before those features activate, and this policy will be updated
first.

Focus Agent helps students plan, start, and focus on their work. It is
deliberately designed NOT to do schoolwork for students, and it declines
requests to write assignments.

## Your choices

- Uninstall anytime; local data is removed with the extension.
- The distraction watcher only runs during sessions you start.
- Annotations and chat history can be cleared from the extension itself.
