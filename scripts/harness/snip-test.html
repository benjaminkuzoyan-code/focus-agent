Photosynthesis (test page)

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. It happens in the chloroplasts, using chlorophyll to capture light.

The overall equation: 6CO2 + 6H2O + light → C6H12O6 + 6O2. The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes; the Calvin cycle runs in the stroma.
