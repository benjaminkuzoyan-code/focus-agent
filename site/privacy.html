Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

The short version: Focus Agent runs on your device. Your assignments, focus sessions, streaks, notes, and chat history stay in your browser's local storage. We don't have a server that collects your data. We can't see your assignments, your grades, or anything else — and we can't sell what we never see.

What Focus Agent reads

  • Your school portal's assignment list (Blackbaud, Canvas, or Google Classroom), using the login already in your browser. This happens on your device. Focus Agent never sees or asks for your portal password.
  • Tab addresses during a focus session you started, checked on your device against a built-in list of distracting sites, so your coach can nudge you. This checking stops when your session ends. Sites you visit are not logged by name anywhere except your own device's session stats.

What Focus Agent stores (on your device only)

Focus sessions and their timing, streaks and XP, per-assignment notes and time estimates, page annotations you draw, your chat messages with the coach, and a cached copy of your assignment list. All of it lives in Chrome's local extension storage. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

What Focus Agent sends off your device

Nothing. The current version has no server, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no accounts.

Students and parents

Focus Agent is built for students, including students under 13. Because the current version collects nothing and has no accounts, there is no personal information for us to hold. If a future version adds accounts or any data collection, we will require verifiable parental consent for users under 13 (COPPA) before those features activate, and this policy will be updated first.

Focus Agent helps students plan, start, and focus on their work. It is deliberately designed not to do schoolwork for students, and it declines requests to write assignments.

Your choices

  • Uninstall anytime; local data is removed with the extension.
  • The distraction watcher only runs during sessions you start.
  • Annotations and chat history can be cleared from the extension itself.
